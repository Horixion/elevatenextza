TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

A fair way
to work together.

We prefer a clear, reciprocal relationship over vague promises or pressure to sell.

Reciprocal referrals

Our preferred starting point is simple: introduce suitable customers to one another when they genuinely need the other service.

  • No exclusivity required.
  • No obligation to refer a minimum number of customers.
  • Each business quotes and contracts for its own work.

Referral fees, if agreed

A referral fee is not automatic. If both businesses want one, agree the amount, qualifying conditions and payment timing in writing before the introduction.

  • No guaranteed earnings.
  • No fee for an unqualified or unpaid enquiry.
  • Customer pricing and scope remain transparent.
We only contact a referred business after it has agreed to the introduction. Please don't send us private customer lists or personal contact details without permission.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A few details before we start a conversation.

Does a partner have to pay to join?

No. There is no joining fee or requirement to buy a service.

Is a referral fee guaranteed?

No. Reciprocal introductions are the starting point. Any fee must be agreed in writing before the referral.

Who manages the website customer?

ElevateNextZA handles website discovery, scope, quotation, design and delivery. The partner remains responsible for their own services.

Can I refer a business without asking first?

Please get the business's permission before sharing its contact details or making an introduction.
